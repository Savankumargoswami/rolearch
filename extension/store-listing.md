# Chrome Web Store listing

**Name:** Rolearch: Job Fit Check

**Summary (132 characters max):**
See how your resume fits any job on LinkedIn, Indeed or a careers page: your match, missing skills and what to fix. Free.

**Category:** Productivity (or Tools)

**Description:**
Looking at a job and not sure you're qualified? Click the Rolearch button.

Rolearch reads the posting you're on and opens it in our free job-fit check, already filled in. Add your resume (or use your Rolearch profile) and see:
- Your match for this job, requirement by requirement, with the evidence from your resume
- The skills and keywords you're missing
- What to fix, and a tailored resume tied to your real experience. Nothing is invented.

Save any job to your Rolearch tracker so every application is in one place.

Works on LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby and most company careers pages. If a page is unusual, select the job description with your mouse and click again.

Free for job seekers.

**Single purpose (for review):**
Send the job posting on the current tab to Rolearch's job-fit check.

**Permission justifications:**
- activeTab: read the job posting on the tab the user is on, only when they click the extension.
- scripting: run the reader on that tab after the click.
- storage: hold the posting for a few seconds while rolearch.com opens, then delete it.
- Content script on rolearch.com/ats: hands the posting to the Rolearch page.

**Data use (privacy form):** collects "Website content" (the job posting the user chose) only to provide the feature; not sold, not used for ads or credit. Privacy policy: https://www.rolearch.com/privacy
